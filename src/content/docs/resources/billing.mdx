Every Varity account has one prepaid credit balance. AI Gateway (Chat, Build,
and API keys) and Varity Cloud (apps, CPU VMs, and GPUs) all spend from it.
**100 credits = $1.**

This page explains the billing shape. The live pricing service and the quote
you confirm own current per-resource prices.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Credits each month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 500 |
| Core | $20 | 2,000 |
| Pro | $100 | 10,000 |
| Business | $500 | 50,000 |
| Enterprise | Contract | Contract |

- Each paid renewal adds the plan's credits to your balance.
- Unused plan credits roll over for **one** billing cycle while your
  subscription stays active. They are lost when you cancel.
- Upgrades take effect immediately and add the difference in credits.
  Downgrades and cancellation take effect at the next billing cycle.
- Paid plans do not also receive the Free plan's 500 credits.

Manage your card, invoices, plan changes, and cancellation from the billing
page of the Developer Portal.

## Free Plan

- **500 credits per month** (UTC calendar month). They do not roll over.
- **Up to 50 credits per day of AI usage**, on the cheapest suitable model.
  Any subscription or top-up removes the daily limit.
- **Static apps** are included.
- Free credits pay only for usage the Free plan includes.

### One Free Starter App

Free accounts can run one **Starter** app at no charge after saving a card
that passes verification. Each card can back only one free app.

- The free app does not spend your 500 credits. Paid plans keep it too.
- It keeps running while you sign in at least once every 30 days. If you do
  not, you get a notice first, and after 30 days without a sign-in the app is
  stopped. Its source is kept, so you can redeploy it in one click.
- It runs on a Varity subdomain.

## Top-Ups

You can buy credits at any time, on any plan.

- Top-ups start at $20, with $100 and $500 packs. You get face value: $20 buys
  2,000 credits. There is no fee and no discount.
- Top-up credits expire 12 months after purchase.
- Auto top-up is optional and off by default. When it is on, you choose the
  threshold, the amount, and a monthly spending cap.

Credits are spent oldest first: plan credits before top-up credits.

<Aside type="caution" title="When credits run out">
A paid action that your balance cannot cover is refused with
`402 insufficient_credits` before anything is started or charged. See
[API Errors](/api/errors/#capacity-and-billing) for every billing code.
</Aside>

## What You're Charged For

Each paid app bills **up to a fixed monthly maximum** in credits, based on the
resources that app reserves, prorated by the time it runs.

Your bill does **not** change with:

- Traffic or bandwidth
- Requests or invocations
- Build minutes

This is the core difference from usage-metered hosting, where billing follows usage meters. With Varity, an unchanged profile keeps the same monthly maximum no matter how much traffic it serves. For the full model, see [How Pricing Works](/resources/pricing/).

CPU VMs and GPUs are priced by the quote you confirm, shown in credits. AI
Gateway requests are charged per request; see
[AI Gateway Billing and Keys](/ai-gateway/billing-and-keys/).

<Aside type="note">
Use the cost calculator in the dashboard (or `varity_cost_calculator` in an AI
editor) for a current estimate before you deploy. The quote shown at
confirmation is the transactional price for that deployment.
</Aside>

## Seeing Your Credits

The billing page of the Developer Portal shows your plan, available credits,
credits that expire soon, the Free plan's daily AI allowance, and your free app
status. Every subscription credit, top-up, charge, refund, and expiry appears
in your billing history.

If billing state cannot be read, the portal and API say it is unavailable
(`503 billing_unavailable`) rather than showing a zero balance.

## Deleting an App

Deleting an app requests teardown. Follow the resulting operation or resource
state until billing reports a terminal stopped state; request acceptance is not
proof that resource release and billing closure are complete.
