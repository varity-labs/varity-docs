Varity billing is built around the profile and resources an app reserves. This
page explains the billing shape; the live pricing service and the quote you
confirm own current numeric values, eligibility, and credits.

## A Card on File

The Developer Portal shows whether the selected workload requires a payment
method before confirmation. Account eligibility and credits come from the live
billing response, not from a rule copied into this page.

## What You're Charged For

Each paid app bills **up to a fixed monthly maximum**, based on the resources that app reserves, prorated by the time it runs. That's it.

Your bill does **not** change with:

- Traffic or bandwidth
- Requests or invocations
- Build minutes

This is the core difference from usage-metered hosting, where billing follows usage meters. With Varity, an unchanged profile keeps the same monthly maximum no matter how much traffic it serves. For the full model, see [How Pricing Works](/resources/pricing/).

<Aside type="note">
Use the cost calculator in the dashboard (or `varity_cost_calculator` in an AI
editor) for a current estimate before you deploy. The quote shown at
confirmation is the transactional price for that deployment.
</Aside>

## Seeing Your Costs

Your dashboard shows the monthly maximum for each app and your overall bill. Because pricing is tied to the reserved profile, there is no end-of-month surprise from a traffic spike.

## Deleting an App

Deleting an app requests teardown. Follow the resulting operation or resource
state until billing reports a terminal stopped state; request acceptance is not
proof that resource release and billing closure are complete.
