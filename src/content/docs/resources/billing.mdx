Every Varity account has one prepaid balance in USD. AI Gateway (Chat, Build,
and API keys) and Varity Cloud (apps, CPU VMs, and GPUs) all spend from it.

This page explains the billing shape. The plan, allowance, and top-up values
below are rendered from a snapshot of the billing contract; the live pricing service and
the quote you confirm own current per-resource prices.

## Plans

<CreditPolicy field="plans" />

- Each paid renewal adds the plan's included usage to your balance.
- Unused plan usage rolls over for <CreditPolicy field="rollover-cycles" />
  while your subscription stays active. It is lost when you cancel.
- Upgrades take effect immediately and add the difference in included usage.
  Downgrades and cancellation take effect at the next billing cycle.
- Paid plans do not also receive the Free plan's monthly usage.

Manage your card, invoices, plan changes, and cancellation from the billing
page of the Developer Portal.

## Free Plan

- **<CreditPolicy field="free-monthly-usage" /> of usage per month** (UTC
  calendar month). It does not roll over.
- **Up to <CreditPolicy field="free-daily-ai-usage" /> of AI usage per day**,
  on the cheapest suitable model. Any subscription or top-up removes
  the daily limit.
- Free usage pays only for <CreditPolicy field="free-promo-categories" />.
  Everything else, including AI Gateway API key requests, needs a paid balance
  from a subscription or top-up.

### One Free Starter App

Free accounts can run one **Starter** app at no charge after saving a card
that passes verification. Each card can back only one free app.

- The free app does not spend your Free plan usage. Paid plans keep it too.
- It keeps running while you sign in at least once every 30 days. If you do
  not, you get a notice first, and after 30 days without a sign-in the app is
  stopped. Its source is kept, so you can redeploy it in one click.
- It runs on a Varity subdomain.

## Top-Ups

You can add funds at any time, on any plan.

- Top-ups start at <CreditPolicy field="top-up-minimum" />, with
  <CreditPolicy field="top-up-presets" /> options.
- <CreditPolicy field="top-up-expiry" />
- Auto top-up is optional and off by default. When it is on, you choose the
  threshold, the amount, and a monthly spending cap.

Your balance is spent oldest first: plan usage before top-ups.

<Aside type="caution" title="When your balance runs out">
A paid action that your balance cannot cover is refused with a `402`
before anything is started or charged. Every billing code is listed in
[API Errors](/api/errors/#capacity-and-billing).
</Aside>

## What You're Charged For

Each paid app bills **up to a fixed monthly maximum** in USD, based on the
resources that app reserves, prorated by the time it runs.

Your bill does **not** change with:

- Traffic or bandwidth
- Requests or invocations
- Build minutes

This is the core difference from usage-metered hosting, where billing follows usage meters. With Varity, an unchanged profile keeps the same monthly maximum no matter how much traffic it serves. For the full model, see [How Pricing Works](/resources/pricing/).

CPU VMs and GPUs are priced by the quote you confirm, shown in USD. AI
Gateway requests are charged per request; see
[AI Gateway Billing and Keys](/ai-gateway/billing-and-keys/).

<Aside type="note">
Use the cost calculator in the dashboard (or `varity_cost_calculator` in an AI
editor) for a current estimate before you deploy. The quote shown at
confirmation is the transactional price for that deployment.
</Aside>

## Seeing Your Balance

The billing page of the Developer Portal shows your plan, available balance,
plan usage that expires soon, the Free plan's daily AI allowance, and your free
app status. Every plan renewal, top-up, charge, refund, and expiry appears
in your billing history.

If billing state cannot be read, the portal and API say it is unavailable
rather than showing a zero balance.

## Deleting an App

Deleting an app requests teardown. Follow the resulting operation or resource
state until billing reports a terminal stopped state; request acceptance is not
proof that resource release and billing closure are complete.
